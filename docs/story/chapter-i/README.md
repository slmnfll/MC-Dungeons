# Chapter I — The Unwritten Kingdom

## Premise

Guild explorers investigate the ruins of the **Aevori**, an ancient civilization whose structures preserve memories as physical Echoes. The Aevori did not collapse: an attempt to transfer the population into a parallel layer called the **Veil** trapped the civilization between reality and memory.

Each restored ruin strengthens the Veil network. Its creator, the **Unwritten King**, intends to overwrite the present world with a perfect reconstruction of his lost kingdom.

## Chapter Direction

- **Tone:** Ancient mystery
- **Players:** Guild explorers
- **Deterioration:** The Veil increasingly changes each region while preserving its native identity.
- **Chapter antagonist:** The Unwritten King
- **Conclusion:** The guild prevents the reconstructed kingdom from replacing the present world and decides the fate of the trapped Aevori.
