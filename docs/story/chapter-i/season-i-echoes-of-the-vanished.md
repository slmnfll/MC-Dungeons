# Season I — Echoes of the Vanished

**Objective:** Establish the guild expedition and determine why an entire civilization disappeared without leaving bodies.

**Build identity:** Pale stone, moss, roots, aged copper, gardens and abandoned civic architecture.

| Level | Dungeon | Theme | Adventure |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Rootbound Gate | Monumental forest-swallowed gate | Open the sealed road into Aevori territory. |
| 20 | The Mossglass Aqueduct | Hanging canals and ruined greenhouses | Restore water and discover channels that react to voices. |
| 40 | The Bellmaker's Ward | Abandoned town and enormous bell towers | Replay the district's final day through its bells. |
| 70 | The Sepulchre of Empty Names | Catacombs and unused tombs | Discover thousands of graves containing no bodies. |
| 100 | The Palace of First Echoes | Ceremonial palace overtaken by roots | Defeat its warden and recover the message: “We did not flee.” |

**Ending:** The guild confirms that the disappearance occurred everywhere at the same moment.
