# Season II — The Drowned Record

**Objective:** Recover the Aevori historical record and learn who deliberately erased the final evidence.

**Build identity:** Dark prismarine, oxidized copper, flooded libraries, coral, canals and sea lanterns.

| Level | Dungeon | Theme | Adventure |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Saltway Locks | Giant canal gates and flooded streets | Reopen the water route into the archival province. |
| 20 | The Scriptorium Below | Submerged writing halls | Recover documents whose final pages were erased. |
| 40 | The Coral Court | Judicial palace transformed into a reef | Reconstruct the trial of scholars who studied the Veil. |
| 70 | The Tidemaker Vault | Pumps, reservoirs and pressure chambers | Expose the chamber hidden beneath the archive. |
| 100 | The Drowned Athenaeum | Colossal underwater library | Recover a forbidden map pointing beyond the known sky. |

**Ending:** The guild learns that the disappearance was concealed before it happened.
