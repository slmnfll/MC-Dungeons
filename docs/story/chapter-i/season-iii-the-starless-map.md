# Season III — The Starless Map

**Objective:** Follow the forbidden map and discover how Aevori astronomers first observed the Veil.

**Build identity:** White stone, calcite, blue glass, snow, high bridges and celestial machinery.

| Level | Dungeon | Theme | Adventure |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Windcarved Stair | Cliffside ascent and ruined lifts | Reach the abandoned astronomical province. |
| 20 | The Monastery of Blue Air | Monastery above the clouds | Decode patterns hidden between the stars. |
| 40 | The Garden of Fallen Stars | Crystal craters and celestial plants | Recover meteor fragments containing memories. |
| 70 | The Meridian Engine | Rotating rings and mechanical planets | Align the engine with the forbidden coordinates. |
| 100 | The Celestial Orrery | Moving planetarium and false constellations | See a city existing beyond physical reality. |

**Ending:** The guild proves that something inside the Veil communicated with the Aevori.
