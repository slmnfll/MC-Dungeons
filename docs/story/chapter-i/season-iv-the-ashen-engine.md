# Season IV — The Ashen Engine

**Objective:** Find and reactivate the machinery that allowed matter to enter the Veil.

**Build identity:** Deepslate, blackstone, copper, lava, rails, furnaces and industrial chambers.

| Level | Dungeon | Theme | Adventure |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cinderrail Terminus | Underground railway and cargo station | Repair transport into the industrial province. |
| 20 | The Brasswork Warrens | Dense maintenance tunnels | Stop machinery that continues building without orders. |
| 40 | The Crucible Gardens | Foundry growing metal-infused plants | Discover the living materials used by Aevori machines. |
| 70 | The Furnace Basilica | Foundry built as a cathedral | Learn that operating the Veil machinery became worship. |
| 100 | The Ashen Engine | City-sized reactor over magma | Restart the engine and unknowingly power the Veil network. |

**Ending:** The restored engine causes the region's deterioration to accelerate.
