# Season IX — The Last Concord

**Objective:** Unite the surviving Echo factions and build a controlled path into the returning capital.

**Build identity:** White stone, gold, dark blue, diplomatic halls, cultural wings and colossal bridges.

| Level | Dungeon | Theme | Adventure |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Embassy of Dust | Diplomatic district of lost provinces | Recover agreements between divided Echo factions. |
| 20 | The Sanctuary of Last Light | Sunlit refuge protected from the Veil | Secure a haven for uncorrupted Echoes. |
| 40 | The Vault of Five Oaths | Five-winged ceremonial vault | Assemble the resistance devices and bind them to the guild. |
| 70 | The Bridge at World's End | Colossal bridge into an incomplete city | Escort allied Echoes while the bridge forms around them. |
| 100 | The Gate of Accord | Monument combining Chapter architecture | Open a controlled entrance into the returning capital. |

**Ending:** The Unwritten King speaks to the guild and invites it into his “perfect” kingdom.
