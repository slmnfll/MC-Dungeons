# Season V — The Missing Hour

**Objective:** Enter a district trapped in the hour of the Vanishing and witness what actually occurred.

**Build identity:** Dark wood, clocks, frozen interiors, repeating streets and rooms caught mid-event.

| Level | Dungeon | Theme | Adventure |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Clockless Borough | Residential streets missing every clock | Follow repeating citizens toward the city center. |
| 20 | The Hall of Repeated Footsteps | Rebuilding government corridors | Reconstruct the final evacuation order. |
| 40 | The Banquet of Still Midnight | Frozen celebration hall | Discover that many citizens expected salvation, not disaster. |
| 70 | The Archive of Tomorrow | Records of events that never occurred | Uncover the perfect future promised by the ruler. |
| 100 | The Palace Between Seconds | Palace fractured across moments | Witness the transfer and the resistance's sabotage. |

**Ending:** The guild learns that some citizens volunteered, while many were forced into the Veil.
