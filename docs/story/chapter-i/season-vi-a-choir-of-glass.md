# Season VI — A Choir of Glass

**Objective:** Contact the consciousness trapped inside the Echoes and learn who controls the Veil network.

**Build identity:** Amethyst, quartz, stained glass, crystal caverns, reflections and musical architecture.

| Level | Dungeon | Theme | Adventure |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Whispering Quarry | Crystal mine filled with conversations | Contact the first coherent trapped mind. |
| 20 | The Resonant Conservatory | Musical academy and instrument halls | Communicate using melodies and repeated sound. |
| 40 | The Gallery of Borrowed Faces | Mirrors and sculpted memory chambers | Help Echoes separate their stolen identities. |
| 70 | The Amphitheatre of Voices | Arena powered by sound | Unite fragmented voices long enough to reveal their ruler's title. |
| 100 | The Cathedral of a Thousand Selves | Cathedral of merged consciousness | Free part of the population and hear “Unwritten King.” |

**Ending:** The trapped Aevori warn that the guild's restorations are allowing their ruler to return.
