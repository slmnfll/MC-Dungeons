# Season VII — The Crownless War

**Objective:** Recover the resistance's history and inherit its unfinished plan for stopping the Unwritten King.

**Build identity:** Fortresses, ruined battlefields, stone brick, iron, trenches, banners and siege machinery.

| Level | Dungeon | Theme | Adventure |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Bastion of Broken Standards | Border fortress and faction banners | Identify the military units that joined the resistance. |
| 20 | The Red Vale Siegeworks | Battlefield, camps and collapsed towers | Relive the battle that disrupted the first transfer. |
| 40 | The Cavalier Necropolis | Tomb-city of resistance commanders | Recover the first device capable of closing the Veil. |
| 70 | The Iron Parliament | Fortified council and war rooms | Discover instructions left for future explorers. |
| 100 | The Crownless Citadel | Mountain fortress around a broken throne | Recover the resistance commander's Echo and final plan. |

**Ending:** The guild formally becomes the continuation of the ancient resistance.
