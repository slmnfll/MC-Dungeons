# Season VIII — Where the Veil Blooms

**Objective:** Stop the Veil from physically replacing the region as the lost capital approaches reality.

**Build identity:** Pale vegetation, impossible skies, inverted terrain, floating debris and unnatural creatures.

| Level | Dungeon | Theme | Adventure |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Orchard of Pale Fruit | Royal orchard of translucent plants | Discover the Veil rewriting living organisms. |
| 20 | The Inverted Mire | Upside-down wetlands and suspended pools | Cross a biome with broken gravity and water flow. |
| 40 | The Menagerie of Unborn Beasts | Research zoo of impossible creatures | Stop abandoned possibilities from becoming living monsters. |
| 70 | The Palace Beneath the Sky | Inverted palace above a void | Destroy an anchor pulling the capital toward reality. |
| 100 | The Blooming Breach | Dimensional wound shaped like a flower | Defeat the King's first complete emissary. |

**Ending:** The breach closes too late; the lost capital begins appearing above the region.
