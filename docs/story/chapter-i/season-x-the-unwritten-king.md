# Season X — The Unwritten King

**Objective:** Enter the returning capital, prevent it from replacing the present world and defeat the Chapter antagonist.

**Build identity:** White and black stone, gold, void spaces, floating streets and corrupted forms of every previous Season.

| Level | Dungeon | Theme | Adventure |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Returning City | Capital materializing over the world | Prevent the overlap from consuming the expedition hub. |
| 20 | The Avenue of Erased Kings | Processional road and faceless statues | Restore the history of rulers erased by the antagonist. |
| 40 | The Labyrinth of Names | Shifting archive of stolen identities | Return names to citizens absorbed into the King. |
| 70 | The Crown Beyond the Veil | Royal sanctum between realities | Destroy the mechanism rewriting physical space. |
| 100 | The Unwritten Throne | Palace whose rooms rewrite themselves | Defeat the King and decide the Aevori's fate. |

**Ending:** The present world survives. Some Aevori regain individuality, some remain within a stabilized Veil and others pass on peacefully.
